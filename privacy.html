Privacy Policy

Scaramouche Bot — Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data Scaramouche Bot ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is stored. We take your privacy seriously and collect only what is necessary for the Bot to function.

2. Data We Collect

The Bot collects and stores the following data when you interact with it:

  • Discord User ID — to identify you across conversations
  • Discord Username & Display Name — to address you correctly
  • Message Content — your messages to the Bot, in servers and in DMs, stored as conversation history to enable memory features
  • Memories derived from your messages — topics you often mention, saved moments ("memory bank"), inside jokes and a short relationship summary
  • User Preferences — mode settings such as unfiltered mode, romance mode and proactive messaging toggles, your time zone, quiet hours and (if you set it with !birthday) your birthday
  • Age confirmation — whether you confirmed you are 18 or older, which unfiltered mode requires
  • Google Docs you edit with the Bot — the document ID and the time you proved you can edit it, when you use !fixdoc
  • Reminders you ask the Bot to keep
  • Channel & Server IDs — to track which channels are active for proactive messaging and to keep each server's conversations apart

What you say in DMs stays out of server channels: commands that quote or profile someone (for example !stalk, !judge or !blackmail) only use what that person said in the same server, server rankings only count that server's messages, and !memories used in a server is answered by DM.

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide conversation memory so the Bot remembers past interactions
  • Store your personal preferences and mode settings
  • Enable proactive messaging features (if opted in)

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses the following third-party APIs to function. Your messages may be processed by these services:

  • Groq — processes your messages, and images or video frames you share with the Bot, to generate responses. See Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • xAI — may analyse images you share with the Bot, when the owner has enabled it. See xAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Fish Audio — processes text to generate voice responses (Google Text-to-Speech is used as a fallback). See Fish Audio's Privacy Policy.
  • DuckDuckGo — receives search queries when the Bot looks something up for you; links you post may be opened to read a preview.
  • Google Docs — documents you ask the Bot to edit with !fixdoc are read and rewritten through Google's API. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Face recognition is only ever used for the Bot owner's own face, at the owner's request.

5. Data Storage

Data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server hosting the Bot. It is not stored in any external database or cloud storage service beyond the hosting platform (Railway).

6. Data Retention

Conversation history is retained indefinitely to enable memory features. You can delete your own data at any time by using the !reset command, which wipes your conversation history from the database.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your conversation history at any time using !reset
  • Opt out of proactive messaging using !proactive off
  • Opt out of voluntary DMs using !dms off
  • Request complete removal of your data by contacting the Bot owner

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the Bot, please contact the Bot owner for data removal.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy concerns or data removal requests, contact the Bot owner through the Discord server where the Bot is hosted.
